The very beautiful crystal clear water, within only a few feet away from you! Villas in Maldives aren't obstructed and the ocean can be seen from anywhere.

So many creatures in the water, especailly sharks. DONT WORRY, it's completely safe and many different type of creatures completely visable from above the water.
